Company Overview

Who we are

R S S Rewinding Service Solution repairs and rewinds electric motors and generators — rebuilding the internal copper coils that make them run, so the equipment works like new again. It's often faster, and considerably cheaper, than replacing it outright.

We've built our reputation on doing this properly: diagnosing the actual problem rather than the obvious one, rewinding and repairing with care, and testing everything before it leaves our workshop. Whether it's one motor holding up your production line or a whole fleet of pumps across your plant, every job gets the same treatment — because we know downtime costs real money.
